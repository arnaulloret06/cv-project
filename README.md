# Arnau Lloret Bota — CV Website

A responsive, single-page curriculum vitae website created for the Hypermedia project at the University of Girona.

## Project files

- `index.html` — page content and section structure.
- `styles.css` — page layout, colours, typography and responsive styles.
- `script.js` — small interaction for the navigation header.
- `foto.jpg` — profile photograph used in the introduction.

## User profile

The website is intended for recruiters, internship coordinators and technology professionals who want to learn about me. The page presents my current studies, technical interests, projects, strengths and languages in clear sections. The intended audience may view the CV on a phone or computer and should be able to find the relevant information quickly. The page  includes section links and a contact button. The content focuses on my third year of Computer Engineering at the University of Girona and on academic and personal projects that demonstrate practical interests.

## Information architecture

The website is a single-page CV. A navigation bar links directly to the About, Education, Skills, Experience and Languages sections. The introduction appears first and identifies my with a photo and the most important information about me. 
- The About section gives a short profile and key facts. 
- Education lists his university degree and previous studies in reverse chronological order. 
- Skills summarises technical foundations, problem solving and collaboration. 
- Experience presents academic projects as evidence of applied learning. 
- Languages and the contact button appear near the end, followed by a compact footer. 
This order gives visitors a quick overview first, then provides supporting details before inviting them to get in touch.

## Visual design

The visual design uses a simple palette of dark green, off-white and a light green accent. Green gives the page a consistent identity and provides strong contrast in the introduction and contact panel. The pale background keeps the longer CV sections comfortable to read. Arial is used as a widely available sans-serif typeface, keeping the page simple and legible. The introduction uses large text to make the name and student role easy to notice, while the rest of the content is divided into cards and clearly titled sections. The profile photograph balances the introduction on larger screens and moves above the text on narrow screens. CSS grid and flexbox organise cards and navigation, with a media query adapting the layout for phones. The design aims to be clear and easy to scan rather than decorative.
